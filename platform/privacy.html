Last Updated: November 19, 2026 • Compliant with GDPR, CCPA, and Global Consumer Privacy Frameworks

1. Who We Are

TechVerse Studio is a web security and certificate automation software platform operated by TechVerse LLC (6346 Cresterrace Dr, Pasadena, TX 77505, USA). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting personal data collected via our website, API endpoints, and WordPress plugins.

2. Secure Payment Processing

All payment transactions on our website are managed and processed securely through industry-standard, PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways (including PayPal and certified merchant acquiring banks). We never store, process, or retain raw credit card numbers or CVV codes on our servers. When you purchase our software, your payment credentials are transmitted directly via 256-bit encrypted SSL/TLS channels to authorized financial networks.

3. Information We Collect

  • Account & Contact Data: Name, email address, company name, and phone number when submitting support requests or licensing software.
  • Technical Diagnostic Data: Target domain names submitted for SSL scanning, public cryptographic certificate expiration dates, and server cipher suite responses. We never inspect private encryption keys or backend server databases.
  • Cookie & Analytics Data: Minimal session cookies necessary for website security, authentication, and load balancing.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use your information exclusively to:

  • Issue and validate digital software licenses and API keys.
  • Deliver real-time SSL expiration alerts and automated security reports.
  • Provide responsive 24/7 technical customer support.
  • Maintain platform security and prevent fraudulent transactions.

5. GDPR & CCPA Consumer Rights

Regardless of your geographic location, you retain the right to:

  • Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or complete deletion of your account and personal records ("Right to be Forgotten").
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email our dedicated privacy officer at support@techversestudio.com. Requests are resolved within 48 business hours.
