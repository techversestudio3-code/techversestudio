1. Information We Collect

TechVerse LLC ("Company", "we") operates techversestudio.com. When you register, monitor domains, or subscribe to our tools, we collect:

  • Contact details: Name and email address.
  • Technical data: Monitored domain hostnames, SSL certificate public metadata, and IP addresses for rate-limiting.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use collected information solely to:

  • Deliver real-time SSL validity scans and automated expiration alert notifications.
  • Provide license keys and updates for the TechVerse SSL Shield WordPress plugin.
  • Improve infrastructure reliability and prevent malicious API exploitation.

3. Third-Party Reseller (Paddle.com)

All checkout and subscription transactions are fulfilled by Paddle.com Market Limited. Paddle operates as an independent Data Controller for customer payment information under GDPR and PCI-DSS Level 1 compliance.

4. Data Security

We implement modern TLS 1.3 encryption, firewalls, and strict access controls. Private keys generated via our client-side CSR utility are created locally in your browser and are never transmitted to our backend.
