At TechVerse Studio, we believe 100% in our software products and plugins. If you are not satisfied with your software subscription or WordPress plugin license within the first 14 days of your initial purchase, we will issue a full refund without hassle.

You may cancel your recurring SaaS subscription (Starter, Professional, or Enterprise) at any time directly through your account dashboard or through the receipt link sent by our reseller Paddle. Upon cancellation, your subscription will remain active until the end of your current billing cycle, after which no further charges will occur.

2. Eligibility for Refunds

Refund requests must be submitted within 14 calendar days from the original transaction timestamp. Refunds apply to:

  • First-time subscription charges for TechVerse Studio SaaS tiers.

3. How to Request a Refund

Because Paddle.com acts as the Merchant of Record for our transactions, you can initiate a refund by:

  1. Contacting Paddle Customer Support directly via the link provided on your email invoice.
  2. Emailing our direct technical support team at support@techversestudio.com with your order number.

4. Processing Time

Approved refunds are processed immediately by Paddle and typically reflect in your original payment method (Credit Card, Debit Card, or PayPal) within 3 to 7 business days, depending on your financial institution.
