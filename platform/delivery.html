In rare cases, automated email filters or spam firewalls might misclassify automated license emails. If you have not received your license within 10 minutes of checkout:

  • Check your Spam, Junk, or Promotions folder for an email from help@paddle.com or support@techversestudio.com.
  • Verify that the email address provided during checkout was spelled accurately.
  • Contact our 24/7 emergency dispatch desk at support@techversestudio.com with your Paddle Order ID for immediate manual re-issuance.
